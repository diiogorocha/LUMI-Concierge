## E-MAIL 2 · Why big families get hit hardest

**Envio:** dia 4
**Assunto A:** Why a family of 8 gets later times
**Assunto B:** What an auto-booking app can't do
**Preview:** A family of 3 needs 3 spots. A family of 8 needs 8.

Hi {{first_name}},

Last time I promised to explain why this gets harder for bigger groups. Here it is.

Every Lightning Lane return time has a limited number of spots.

A family of 3 needs 3 of them at the same time. A family of 8 needs 8.

So when you're traveling with grandparents, cousins or another family, the early times run out for your group faster. That's how big groups end up with the longest gaps between rides, even when the person booking is doing everything right.

**So what actually fixes it?**

Not a better spreadsheet.

What works is someone who keeps checking all day, grabs an earlier slot the moment one opens, keeps your must-do rides first, and books the next ride close to where you already are. Then does it again after the next ride. And the next. For 6 or 7 hours.

You may have seen auto-booking apps that do part of this. They can be useful. But an app books. It doesn't decide.

It doesn't know that your 4-year-old just got scared on a dark ride and needs something gentle next. It doesn't know a ride closed at 11 and your whole morning needs a new order. It doesn't know grandma needs to sit down.

That part takes a person.

**Want to see how a live park day works, step by step?** Just reply to this email with the word **HOW** and I'll send it to you.
