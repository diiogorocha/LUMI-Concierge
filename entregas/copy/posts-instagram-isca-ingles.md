The goal isn't a perfect schedule. It's no dead time between rides.

## POST 4 · Carrossel comparativo: "App vs. human"

**Palavra-chave:** **PLAN**
**Formato:** carrossel 4:5, 7 telas
[Sem citar nenhuma empresa ou app pelo nome. Não usar "bot" como promessa nem "hack".]

| Tela | Texto |
| --- | --- |
| 1 (capa) | **Auto-booking app vs. a real person: what an app can't do on park day** |
| 2 | **What an auto-booking app does well:** watches for return times and books them for you in the background. For some families, that's enough. |
| 3 | **What it can't do: decide.** It doesn't know which ride your kids would cry about missing, and which one is just a bonus. |
| 4 | **What it can't do: react.** A ride closes at 11. It starts to rain. Your 4-year-old got scared and needs something gentle next. |
| 5 | **What it can't do: think about distance.** A slot on the other side of the park isn't a win if it costs you 20 minutes of walking with a stroller. |
| 6 | **A person can.** Keep your priorities first, re-book earlier after every ride, keep the next ride close by, and change the plan with you in the group chat. |
| 7 | The app books. A person runs the day. Want to see what the day really takes? Comment **PLAN** for the free guide 💬 |

[NOTA tela 5: "20 minutes" é ilustrativo. Se preferir evitar qualquer número, usar "a long walk with a stroller".]

**Legenda:**

Auto-booking apps can be useful. This isn't a "they're bad" post.

But there's a difference between *booking* a ride and *running* a park day.

Booking is finding a time.
Running the day is knowing what matters most to your family, reacting when the park changes the plan, and keeping the next ride close so nobody is crossing the park for a time slot.

That second part is what my free guide is about. It walks you through the Family Must-Do List, booking day, and the "re-booking game" that keeps dead time out of your day.

**Formato:** Reels 9:16, 25–35 s, gravação de tela de animação simples (quadro branco digital ou slides animados), sem rosto e sem fala

| 3 | 8–13 s | A família de 3 cabe nas vagas restantes | A family of 3 needs **3 spots at the same time.** Easier to find. |
| 4 | 13–18 s | A família de 8 não cabe e é "empurrada" para uma fileira mais à direita (mais tarde) | A family of 8 needs **8.** The early rows fill up first, so the group gets pushed later. |

Traveling with grandparents, cousins or another family? This is why your times always seem later.

Every return time only has so many spots. A family of 3 needs 3 at the same time. A family of 8 needs 8. The early times run out for big groups first.

The fix isn't a better spreadsheet. It's someone checking and moving your group earlier, over and over, all day.
