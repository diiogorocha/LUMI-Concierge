**Legenda:**

Booking your first 3 Lightning Lanes at 7 AM feels like the hard part.

It's actually the easy part.

After those 3, your next booking only opens once you scan in at a ride. Meanwhile:
↳ the later it gets, the later the available times
↳ the park keeps filling up
↳ rides far apart push everything later
↳ and some of the most-wanted rides run out early

That's how a Disney World day turns into Lightning Lanes mixed with standby lines, long gaps between rides, and the one ride everyone wanted, missed.

The goal isn't a perfect schedule. It's no dead time between rides.

## POST 4 · Carrossel comparativo: "App vs. human"

**Palavra-chave:** **PLAN**
**Formato:** carrossel 4:5, 8 telas
[Sem citar nenhuma empresa ou app pelo nome. Não usar "bot" como promessa nem "hack".]

| Tela | Texto |
| --- | --- |
| 1 (capa) | **The Lightning Lane your kids want most can run out before you've used your first one.** · *What an app can't do about it, and what a person can* |
| 2 | **What an auto-booking app does well:** watches for return times and books them for you in the background. For some families, that's enough. |
| 3 | **What it can't do: know what runs out.** Some of the most-wanted rides are gone early. Knowing which ones, on which days, comes from being in the parks every day. |
| 4 | **What it can't do: decide.** It doesn't know which ride your kids would cry about missing, and which one is just a bonus. |
| 5 | **What it can't do: react.** A ride closes at 11. It starts to rain. Your 4-year-old got scared and needs something gentle next. |
| 6 | **What it can't do: think about distance.** A slot on the other side of the park isn't a win if the long walk with a stroller pushes everything else later. |
| 7 | **A person can.** Go after the rides that run out first, book the next one the moment it's possible, keep the next ride close, and change the plan with you in the group chat. |
| 8 | The app books. A person runs the day. Want to see what the day really takes? Comment **PLAN** for the free guide 💬 |

**Legenda:**

Auto-booking apps can be useful. This isn't a "they're bad" post.

But there's a difference between *booking* a ride and *running* a park day.

Booking is finding a time.
Running the day is knowing which rides run out early, what matters most to your family, reacting when the park changes the plan, and keeping the next ride close so nobody is crossing the park for a time slot.

Our team is in the parks every day, so we see which rides tend to go first. That second part is what our free guide is about: the Family Must-Do List, booking day, and the "re-booking game" that keeps dead time out of your day.

## POST 5 · Reels com quadro branco digital: "Family of 3 vs. family of 8"

**Palavra-chave:** **PREP**
**Formato:** Reels 9:16, 25–35 s, gravação de tela de animação simples (quadro branco digital ou slides animados), sem rosto e sem fala
[Desenho genérico, **sem horários reais**. Rotular "Illustration" na tela 2.]

| Tela | Tempo | Visual | Texto na tela |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | 0–3 s | Dois grupos de bonequinhos: 3 e 8 | **Family of 3 vs. family of 8: why your Lightning Lane times keep getting later** |
| 2 | 3–8 s | Uma "fileira" de vagas para um horário (quadradinhos), alguns já preenchidos | Each return time has a limited number of spots. *(Illustration)* |
| 3 | 8–13 s | A família de 3 cabe nas vagas restantes | A family of 3 needs **3 spots at the same time.** Easier to find. |
| 4 | 13–18 s | A família de 8 não cabe e é "empurrada" para uma fileira mais à direita (mais tarde) | A family of 8 needs **8.** The early rows fill up first, so the group gets pushed later. |
| 5 | 18–23 s | Relógio andando; as fileiras da esquerda vão se apagando | And the later it gets, the fewer early spots are left for anyone. |
| 6 | 23–28 s | Seta voltando para a esquerda; uma vaga abre e o grupo "pula" para mais cedo | The fix: book the next ride the moment you can, and **move the group earlier** whenever spots open up. |
| 7 | 28–33 s | Fundo da marca | After every ride. For 6–7 hours. Big group? Comment **PREP** for the free guide |

**Legenda:**

Traveling with grandparents, cousins or another family? This is why your times always seem later.

Every return time only has so many spots. A family of 3 needs 3 at the same time. A family of 8 needs 8. The early times run out for big groups first, and the later it gets, the fewer are left.

The fix isn't a better spreadsheet. It's someone booking the next ride the moment it's possible and moving your group earlier, over and over, all day.
